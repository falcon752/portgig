Project Insight

Creative Direction

The brief was to capture the essence of urban solitude. We focused on high-contrast lighting and minimalist compositions to evoke a sense of introspection amidst the city's energy.

Technical Execution

Utilized a 50mm f/1.2 lens for shallow depth of field, isolating subjects from their environment. Post-production involved desaturation and split-toning to achieve a timeless, cinematic feel.
