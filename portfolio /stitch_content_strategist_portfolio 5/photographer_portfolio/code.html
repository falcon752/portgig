Project Insight

Client Brief & Creative Direction

The goal was to create a series of portraits for a tech startup's 'Our Team' page. The brief called for images that were professional yet approachable, breaking away from stiff corporate headshots. Creative direction focused on capturing each individual's personality in their natural workspace, using soft, natural light to create a warm and inviting atmosphere.

Technical Execution & Impact

Executed with a Canon R5 and a 50mm f/1.2L lens to achieve a shallow depth of field, isolating subjects from their background. The final images boosted the company's website engagement and were praised for their authenticity, helping to humanize the brand and make the team more relatable to potential clients and new hires.
